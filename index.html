Learn Quant Trading with Phoebe

A data-science course about the one experiment that cannot be rerun. A backtest is one realised path of history, read once, by somebody who tried many rules on it and brought you the best. Strategy search is a multiple-testing problem; the market is adversarial, reflexive and non-stationary, so a signal decays once it is found; the signal-to-noise ratio is near zero; leakage has classes of its own; costs erase small edges; risk is the product. Every number is measured on a generator where the truth is a dial: the best of 100 random rules reports a Sharpe ratio of 1.80 on nothing and earns nothing afterwards, the deflated test lets through zero false discoveries and, on two years of data, zero real ones too, and a shuffled split lifts a rule with no information to 0.65. Six leader sessions on the decision, ten practitioner sessions on the build. Synthetic data only, no market series, no tickers, no prices, and nothing here is investment advice.

If you come from a data team

Leader session 6 in three columns. What a conventional analyst, engineer or scientist already has that is worth more here, what is new, and what is actively dangerous.

Carries over

  • Multiple-testing instincts: Bonferroni, false discovery rates, the habit of asking how many were tried
  • Pipeline hygiene: seeds, reproducibility, a reference implementation that must agree to a stated precision
  • Leakage hunting on a timeline, from feature engineering and model validation
  • Reading a result as a distribution over trials rather than a single run

Must learn

  • The Sharpe ratio and its standard error: about 0.71 on two years of daily data with nothing there
  • The expected maximum of N trials and the deflated Sharpe ratio (Bailey and Lopez de Prado 2014)
  • Purged and embargoed walk-forward, and why overlapping observations make a shuffled split leak
  • Costs as turnover times basis points, and sizing where the mean and the median part ways

Must unlearn

  • Shuffled k-fold as the default split; on overlapping observations it reports a finding on nothing
  • A good holdout score as evidence; the holdout is consumed by being read and cannot be redrawn
  • Reporting the mean path; at leverage 12 the mean is 71 and the median is 1.45
  • Treating the series as a customer who holds still; the signal decays once it is found and traded

What is real here

Every number was computed before any page quoted it, on a generator whose truth is a dial. The algorithms run over it are the ordinary ones.

The selectionN random rules scored on 503 days, the best kept and scored on the next 252. 200 worlds per rung. The best of 100 on nothing: 1.80 in-sample, -0.06 out
The haircutThe probabilistic and deflated Sharpe ratio from the 2014 paper, implemented from its equations and checked against its own Python snippet. Zero false discoveries on every planted rung
The splitWalk-forward, blocked and shuffled folds with a purge and an embargo. Shuffled on five-day holds: 0.65 on nothing. Purged: -0.02
The engine checks itselfSix properties on every page load; one fails when the purge is made a no-op. A Python reference reproduces all 635 canon numbers to 3.3e-13
